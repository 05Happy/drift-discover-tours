6 nights / 7 days · your journey, day by day

Seven days to
drift away.

Every transfer and major sight is planned, leaving you with more time to be present.

01

Arrival in Port Blair & City Tour

After arriving at Port Blair airport, you will be welcomed and transferred to your hotel for check-in and time to settle in. After lunch, discover the historic Cellular Jail and unwind by the sea at Corbyn's Cove Beach. In the evening, return to Cellular Jail for its moving Light & Sound Show before an overnight stay in Port Blair.

Port Blair
02

Ross & North Bay Island Excursion

Enjoy breakfast before leaving by boat for a full island excursion. Visit North Bay Island, where snorkeling and scuba diving can be arranged as optional activities, then explore historic Ross Island - officially Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose Island. Return to your hotel in Port Blair at approximately 4-5 PM and relax overnight.

Port Blair
03

Port Blair to Havelock Island

Begin early, departing your hotel around 5 or 6 AM with a packed breakfast. Board the cruise for Havelock Island, check in to your hotel on arrival, and later head to the famous Radhanagar Beach. Spend the evening soaking in its wide sands and island atmosphere before an overnight stay in Havelock.

Havelock
04

Havelock to Neil Island

After breakfast, take the cruise onward to peaceful Neil Island and check in at your hotel. The day's island sightseeing includes Bharatpur Beach, known for its clear water, followed by Laxmanpur Beach, a beautiful location to enjoy the sunset. Stay overnight on Neil Island.

Neil Island
05

Natural Bridge & Return to Port Blair

After breakfast, visit Neil Island's much-loved Natural Bridge, a remarkable coastal rock formation best appreciated during low tide. Later, board the ferry back to Port Blair. The evening is kept for shopping at Sagarika Emporium before returning to your hotel for an overnight stay.

Port Blair
06

Baratang Island & Limestone Caves

This adventure starts very early - around 3 AM from the hotel - with a packed breakfast for the journey. Drive through the protected Jarawa Reserve Forest, then visit Baratang's famous Limestone Caves. Return to Port Blair by the afternoon convoy, reaching your hotel at roughly 4 PM, followed by your final overnight stay in Port Blair.

Port Blair
07

Departure from Port Blair

Enjoy your final morning at leisure before checking out as per hotel timings. You will be dropped at Port Blair airport according to your flight schedule, taking home wonderful memories of beaches, ferries, island sunsets, and a week in the Andamans.

Before you travel

Important tour
information.

We want you to travel with complete clarity. Please keep the following important points in mind while planning your Andaman holiday.

  • All tours are subject to weather conditions. The programme may be changed, altered, or extended if unavoidable circumstances arise.
  • Shared vessels, including ships, boats, and ferries, will be arranged as required. If private ferry or cruise seats are unavailable, guests will travel by government ferry.
  • Rooms are allotted family-wise. Standard check-out time is generally between 7:30 AM and 8:30 AM; early check-in and late check-out depend on availability and are chargeable when confirmed.
  • Any increase in ferry tickets, entry fees, road transport fares, or similar charges must be borne by the guest.
  • Hotel check-in and check-out policies apply individually at each property. Confirmed early arrivals or extended stays require prior hotel approval.
  • Please follow local guidance throughout the trip, especially during ferry travel, beach visits, and the Baratang forest route.
